Generative AI & RAG

LangChain RAG AI Fee & Query Chatbot

Jul 2026 • 150 Words

The Problem

Students and university administrative staff spent hours manually looking up fee breakdowns, payment deadlines, and scholarship eligibility across complex multi-tab Excel files, causing support bottlenecks.

Technical Approach

Engineered an end-to-end Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system using LangChain, Hugging Face embeddings, and python-dotenv. Parsed structured Excel spreadsheets into vectorized document chunks, enabling semantic search and context injection before generating responses with Hugging Face LLMs in a Streamlit frontend.

Measurable Result

Reduced average student query lookup latency to <1.5 seconds with 100% extraction precision against official fee structures, completely eliminating model hallucination on numeric financial queries.

Student Stress Level Predictive System

Jul 2026 • 140 Words

The Problem

Academic pressure, sleep deprivation, and lifestyle imbalances severely impact student performance, but early warning indicators are rarely caught before academic burnouts occur.

Technical Approach

Developed a supervised multi-class classification model using Scikit-Learn. Preprocessed feature vectors (sleep duration, study hours, social media usage, exam stress, attendance) with StandardScaler. Evaluated decision trees, KNN, and random forest classifiers, deploying the optimal pipeline via a Streamlit web app.

Measurable Result

Achieved over 94% cross-validated classification accuracy, providing instant automated stress risk categorization (Low / Medium / High) with actionable lifestyle suggestions.
